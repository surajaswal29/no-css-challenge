A few quiet answers

Why no CSS?

Because HTML alone is more capable than most people remember. Colour, layout, type, motion (through SVG) and even interactive widgets are all built into the language.

Why no JavaScript?

Every page here works the moment it arrives. Nothing to download, parse or hydrate, so it is fast on slow phones and slow connections.

What is SMIL?

The animation language built into SVG. The drifting clouds and the breathing circle are declared with <animate> elements, not scripts or stylesheets.

Can I copy this?

Yes. View the source of any page (Ctrl+U or ⌘+⌥+U) and take whatever you like.

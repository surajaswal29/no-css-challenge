About this blog

Why a web developer would choose to write with one hand tied behind their back.

The story

This blog began as a challenge: could a website look calm and considered using only HTML? No stylesheet, no script, nothing but the language the web was first written in.

It turns out the answer is yes, and the constraint is a gift. Pages load instantly, read well on any device, and are simple enough to understand by viewing their source.

A small word
The Japanese word 静か(shizuka) means quiet or calm. It is the mood this site aims for, and the <ruby> element that shows its reading is pure HTML too.

What I believe

  1. Fast is kind. A page that loads instantly respects people on old phones and slow connections.

  2. Structure first. Good HTML is accessible before a single style is added.

  3. Less, but better. Every element should earn its place.

  4. Share what you learn. Everything here is free to read, copy and improve.

Questions

Is HTML-only styling allowed?

It is. Attributes such as bgcolor, cellpadding and <font> are obsolete for authors, but the HTML standard still tells browsers how to render them, so they work everywhere.

Does this work on phones?

Yes. The reading column is a table cell that asks for 720 pixels and gives way on smaller screens, and every illustration is a scalable SVG.

How often do you post?

Slowly. When there is something worth saying.

Colophon

Type
Georgia, or the closest serif your device has. No web fonts to download.
Colour

FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

How much does a project cost?

Most projects land between $12k and $60k. After the intro call we send a fixed quote, and that is the price you pay.

Do you work with businesses outside your time zone?

Yes. About half our clients are overseas. We overlap at least three working hours with every client.

Who owns the code and designs?

You do, in full, once the final invoice is paid. We keep nothing back and use no lock-in.

Can I buy a product without a project?

Of course. All products are sold on their own, with a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Have something in mind?

Tell us about your business and what is slowing it down. We reply within one working day.
